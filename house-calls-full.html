Why House Calls

You should not need a grandchild on speed dial.

Somewhere along the way, everyday life moved onto screens. The bank, the doctor, the pharmacy, the photos of the grandkids. Nobody asked whether that worked for you, and nobody came to the house to explain it.

House Calls is the person who comes to the house. We sit down with you, fix what is bothering you, set things up so they are safe and simple, and write it all down in large print before we leave. If you live far away, we do the same thing by phone and by sharing your screen.

For you

You run your own life and intend to keep doing it. You would just like fewer passwords, fewer pop-ups, and fewer phone calls from strangers.

For your mom or dad

You are the family help desk, and you live three states away. We become the patient person nearby, and we keep you in the loop.

For anyone who says "I'm not a computer person"

No jargon and no judgment. If we use a word you do not know, stop us. That one is on us.

Good questions

Things people ask first.

Do you keep my passwords?

No. Your passwords go into a password manager that belongs to you and that only you can open. You type them in yourself. We never write them in our notes and we never ask you to read one aloud.

Will you try to sell me something?

No. We do not sell phones, computers or subscriptions, and we take no commissions from anyone. If you would be better off with different equipment or a cheaper plan, we will say so and you can buy it wherever you like.

I am really not good with this. Is that a problem?

That is exactly who this is for. We go at your pace, we repeat things as often as you like, and we leave everything written down in large print. Nobody is graded.

My mother does not think she needs help. How do I bring it up?

Start with one annoyance, not with "you need help." Give a single House Call for the one thing she has complained about, such as the remote or the printer. We take it from there, at her pace. And we work for her: she decides what is shared with you.

Can you help if we do not live near you?

Yes. Most of what we do works well by phone and by sharing your screen. We walk you through starting the screen share, you can see everything we do, and you can end it at any moment. We never connect when you are not there.

What happens with my bank accounts?

We can sit next to you while you set up online banking, alerts and bill pay. You type your own passwords and you press every button that moves money. We also suggest adding a trusted contact at your bank, which is a person the bank may call if something looks wrong.

Is it safe to use AI?

With sensible settings and a few simple rules, yes. We set it up, practice with you, and leave a card that says what never to type into it. An AI assistant never gets your passwords or your bank logins. And when what you need is a person, you get a person.

Do you use AI in your own work?

Yes, and we say so. We use it to help draft your guides and notes and to research your exact devices. Brooks reads every word before it reaches you. Your passwords, account numbers and Social Security number never go into it.

How fast do you answer?

Messages sent before 6 pm on a weekday get an answer the same day. If something feels urgent, such as a caller pressing you to act right now, hang up first. Then send it to us.

Can I give this as a gift?

Yes. A House Call or a Safe and Simple setup makes a real gift for a parent or grandparent. Ask for a printed gift certificate, and we will schedule the visit with them directly.
