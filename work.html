Media / AI Platform
Claude Vision Whisper MCP Server Retrieval Python

Turning a video archive into an AI platform

Before

A video production team had accumulated 5,900+ files with no consistent naming, patchy tagging, and virtually no usable metadata. Finding the right clip meant hours of manual searching — or giving up and reshooting something they already owned.

After

We built a platform, not a filing system. Claude Vision tags every video by client, sector, and type. Whisper transcription with word-level timestamps makes the archive searchable by what was actually said on camera, and jumps straight to the cut point. A custom MCP server puts the entire library in front of Claude, so the team queries it from Slack and assembles rough cuts from a written brief in minutes. It runs unattended, with monitoring and alerting.

5,900+ Videos tagged & transcribed
6 Weeks to full completion
100% Searchable by transcript
Publishing / AI Product
Claude Next.js Supabase Vercel

Giving a bestselling creativity deck an AI brain

Before

Roger von Oech's Creative Whack Pack has sold over a million copies since 1988, and its iOS app has been in the App Store since 2009. The cards work when you sit down with them, but a card can only ask a question. It cannot hear your specific problem and apply the strategy back to it. The app had not had a new idea in years.

After

With Roger's blessing, and his original card text and art untouched, we built the deck a thinking partner. Name a problem, pick a door (Do a Workshop, Ask the Oracle, A Reading, or Pick Your Own Cards), and the AI deals cards from all four suits and applies each strategy to what you actually said. A one-card conversation mode asks the card's own questions one at a time before delivering the whack. Every session gets a shareable link and a printable summary. The first version was live eleven days after the first email. Seven versions shipped in the six weeks after, each one shaped by Roger's notes.

100 Strategy cards, one AI brain
11 Days from first email to live product
7 Versions shipped in six weeks
