Good questions

Things people ask first.

Do you keep my passwords?

No. Your passwords go into a password manager that belongs to you and that only you can open. You type them in yourself. We never write them in our notes and we never ask you to read one aloud.

Will you try to sell me something?

No. We do not sell phones, computers or subscriptions, and we take no commissions from anyone. If you would be better off with different equipment or a cheaper plan, we will say so and you can buy it wherever you like.

My mother does not think she needs help. How do I bring it up?

Start with one annoyance, not with "you need help." Give a single House Call for the one thing she has complained about, such as the remote or the printer. We take it from there, at her pace. And we work for her: she decides what is shared with you.

Can you help if we do not live near you?

Yes. Most of what we do works well by phone and by sharing your screen. You can see everything we do, and you can end it at any moment. We never connect when you are not there.

What do you not do?

We do not give legal, tax, medical or investment advice, and we do not repair broken hardware. We will tell you plainly when something belongs with your attorney, accountant, doctor or a repair shop.

Do you use AI in your own work?

Yes, and we say so. We use it to help draft your guides and notes and to research your exact devices. Brooks reads every word before it reaches you. Your passwords, account numbers and Social Security number never go into it.

Can I give this as a gift?

Yes. A House Call or a Safe and Simple setup makes a real gift for a parent or grandparent. Ask for a printed gift certificate, and we will schedule the visit with them directly.

Do you give talks to groups?

Yes, free for libraries, senior centers and congregations. Favorites are Scam-Proof in an Hour, Passwords Without Tears, and Meet Your AI Assistant. Large-print handouts included.
