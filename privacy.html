Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Attic Light Solutions ("we," "us") provides AI consulting and automation services, including custom software tools and integrations built for individual clients. This policy describes how we handle information across our website and those client tools.

Information we collect

  • Website: If you contact us through the site, we collect the information you provide (name, email, message). We do not run advertising trackers.
  • Client tools and integrations: Software we build for clients may access business data from systems the client explicitly connects (for example, accounting, payroll, point-of-sale, or e-commerce platforms). Access is granted by the client through each platform's own authorization flow and can be revoked by the client at any time.

How we use information

  • To respond to inquiries and provide contracted services.
  • To generate reports, analysis, and operational insights for the client whose data it is.
  • We use client business data solely on behalf of that client. We do not sell data, share it with third parties for marketing, or use one client's data for any other client.

Storage and security

Connection credentials are stored using platform-standard authorization tokens, never shared passwords, and are kept out of source control. Data is retained only as long as needed to provide the contracted service, and access is limited to the client and Attic Light Solutions personnel working on that client's engagement.

Third-party platforms

Client tools may connect to third-party services (such as Intuit QuickBooks, Shopify, or Google Workspace). Your use of those platforms is governed by their own privacy policies. We access only the data scopes each integration requires.

Your choices

Clients may disconnect any integration or request deletion of stored data at any time by contacting us.
